## Interface (the contract other code relies on)

- Water tiles are optional `lod_<quads>/chunk_<i>_<j>/water_u16.bin` files containing `(quads + 1)^2` little-endian uint16 samples, nearest-neighbour sampled from the 2001 × 2001 water level map. Zero means no water. `tiles/water_index.json` lists the chunks with water per LOD.
- `terrain.waterSurfaceAt(x,z)` returns surface elevation or `null`; `terrain.waterDepthAt(x,z)` returns non-negative depth or 0; `terrain.distanceToRiverAt(x,z)` returns metres or `null`.
- `terrain.isWaterAt(x,z)` identifies generated lake / wide-river water. `terrain.rivers` contains river reach records from `rivers.json`.
- Controls use the surface/depth adapter for normal speed in water up to 0.4 m, 60% speed between 0.4 and 1.2 m, and block movement into deeper water. The HUD reports nearest-river distance, depth, wading, or blocked crossing.
- Water geometry is built per terrain chunk, at most LOD32, reuses materials, and is disposed with the terrain chunk. Narrow-river ribbons are limited to the configured quality radius.
- The standalone map overlay uses a cached far-grid background, lakes and class-width river strokes; each frame redraws only the player marker/viewport. It is toggled with N (minimap) and M (full map).
